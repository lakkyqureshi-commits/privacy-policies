# Privacy Policy for Indian Voice Lovers Player

**Effective Date:** 22 September 2026

## 1. Media Access
This app requires access to your device's storage solely for local playback. We do not upload your personal media files.

## 2. Accessibility and TTS
The app uses Android's Text-to-Speech engine for accessibility purposes. No voice data is transmitted to our servers.

## 3. Data Collection
This app does not collect personal user data. All settings are saved locally.
